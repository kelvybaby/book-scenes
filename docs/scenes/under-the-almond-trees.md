# Scene specification: Under the Almond Trees

## Status

Visual direction approved. [Study O](../../design/concepts/under-the-almond-trees-study-o-gridlocked-face-v13.png) is the implementation reference; responsive framing, layer separation, typography, and interaction remain to be proven in the browser.

## Spoiler boundary

Use only material from the early portion already read: through Fermina's forced journey and passage through the relatives' village described by the reader. Do not introduce later plot developments, symbols, relationships, locations, or retrospective interpretations.

## Narrative moment

Treat the scene as a compressed memory tableau of the early courtship, not a claim that every depicted element occupied one literal instant. It combines the first sight at Fermina's house, Florentino's repeated presence in the park, Fermina's awareness of him, and the charged distance preceding their correspondence. Exact visual details should be reconciled with descriptive passages supplied by the reader.

## Narrative purpose

Create a place organized around partial sight, correspondence, thresholds, and the distance between two young people. The visitor occupies the relationship between park and house rather than Florentino's body. Both Fermina and Florentino are consciously visible but remain subordinate to the place. Fermina is slightly more legible at the doorway without becoming a portrait.

The scene should be inviting enough to explore and emotionally ambiguous enough not to celebrate obsession uncritically.

## Source-derived visual anchors

The first studies should preserve these details supplied from the text while avoiding a literal illustration of one single instant:

- One of the oldest houses on the park, visibly half-ruined rather than picturesquely pristine
- An abbey-cloister quality: arches, a passageway, an interior patio, and a window crossed by a passerby
- Neglected flowerpots, weeds, and a dry stone fountain
- A bright patio whose life is first registered through a woman's voice
- Almond trees providing shade, yellow falling leaves, and a concealed park bench
- Dry-season heat, deserted streets, wind-driven dead leaves, and no lush tropical rain sheen
- Fermina's blue-striped school uniform and single braid remain contextual evidence from an earlier glimpse
- For the selected composite moment, Fermina sits alone at the doorway beneath the almond trees in a narrow, many-folded Greek-style tunic and a fresh-gardenia garland
- Florentino is very thin and timid-looking, with flattened dark hair, myopic eyeglasses, and an inherited black suit made oppressive by the heat; these details govern any visible fragment rather than requiring a recognizable depiction

These anchors are visual evidence, not a requirement to show every detail at once.

## Early decay motif

Decay is already present here through material neglect and seasonal dryness: ruined plaster, empty water infrastructure, weeds, dead leaves, heat, and deserted streets. Set these against the astonishing brightness and living voice of the patio. The scene should suggest beauty persisting inside neglect, not apply a generalized gothic filter.

Later scenes may develop social, ecological, bodily, moral, and mnemonic decay when the relevant text supports them. Do not foreshadow later meanings so strongly that this young scene appears retrospective or spoiler-laden.

## Visitor experience

1. Arrive at a complete, almost-still late-afternoon tableau.
2. Notice small physical signs of life and distance.
3. Discover that selected objects respond with restraint.
4. Open passages through objects whose narrative role suits the language.
5. Return to the same scene state after reading.
6. Eventually recognize the road leaving the square as a path toward a later scene.

No tutorial overlay, score, mandatory order, or glowing hotspot map.

## Camera and framing

- View from the park toward Fermina's house.
- Use a wider third-person view set apart from Florentino; never frame the scene over his shoulder.
- Place Florentino and his bench in the left or left-middle foreground/midground. His complete seated silhouette may be visible, but it remains small relative to the house and trees.
- Vegetation frames rather than hides the principal architecture.
- The house sits across a breathable middle distance.
- The doorway beneath the almond trees holds a small, partial view of Fermina seated alone under falling yellow leaves.
- The view is slightly oblique and observed, not a perfectly symmetrical elevation.
- Neither face becomes a focal portrait. Small identifying details may resolve on inspection, while architecture, light, leaves, and negative space dominate the first reading.

## Depth layers

### Far atmosphere

- Dry-season late-afternoon glare softening toward evening
- Distant roofs, towers, bells, or balconies used sparingly
- Heat haze and tonal compression

### House

- Warm weathered façade
- Human-scaled, irregular, and residential rather than monumental or palatial
- Shutters, doors, courtyard depth, and iron or wood details
- A doorway shaded by almond branches, with room for Fermina to sit naturally at the threshold
- Two chairs at the threshold: Fermina occupies one while the empty chair faces her and retains the aunt's absence
- Layered arches and passageways leading toward the bright patio without turning the façade into a grand civic arcade
- The dry stone fountain belongs inside the visible patio, never in the park or street
- Telegraph wire crossing or leaving the composition
- One future evening light source

### Square and park

- A legible park boundary made by curb, low planting, path, or change of ground material
- Worn paths, ground texture, trunks, and planting that integrate the bench into the park
- A distinct street separates the park from the house and remains visibly crossable
- Evidence of the city without crowding the emotional axis

### Foreground

- Almond foliage and an open view into the park
- Florentino's bench and partly open book at a comfortable camera distance
- Concealed letter possibility
- Leaves, flowers, cloth, or shadow masking layer boundaries
- Florentino consciously visible as a slender, indistinct seated silhouette

## Figures

- Do not begin with detailed, front-facing portraits.
- Both Fermina and Florentino must be present in the first frame, but neither should initially read as a character portrait.
- Fermina should be small and partially screened by doorway shadow, almond leaves, or architectural depth. Her seated posture, folded tunic, separate gardenia blossoms, embroidery, and clean downcast profile distinguish her without turning her into a portrait. Do not include the aunt.
- Fermina continues embroidering with her gaze lowered; an empty facing chair implies the aunt who has just left without depicting her.
- Florentino should be consciously visible as a complete or nearly complete seated silhouette at a distance. His bowed head, narrow black-suited posture, book, and perhaps a tiny eyeglass glint may read, but not his facial features or identifying detail.
- Do not enlarge Florentino into the camera viewpoint or reduce him to a disembodied hand and book.
- Narrative balance comes from the visual path between the bench and doorway, not equal size or descriptive detail.
- Human scale and period clothing must remain consistent across concept studies.
- Reject distorted anatomy, implausible poses, or faces that become the scene's accidental focal point.

## Lighting progression

Provisional idea: a very slow late-afternoon-to-blue-hour transition over several minutes.

Possible changes:

- Shadows lengthen and cool.
- Architectural light becomes warmer by contrast.
- A lamp or window begins to glow.
- Insects gather near visible light.
- Passage paper becomes more luminous against its surroundings.

Do not implement the cycle until the static composition works. Omit it if it competes with reading or looks like a filter transition.

## Passage presentation

- Passage text is typeset in HTML and remains selectable.
- Preserve the supplied text exactly.
- On desktop, anchor the reveal spatially to its object while maintaining a comfortable reading measure.
- Quiet or temporarily desaturate competing motion while a passage is open.
- Provide an explicit close action, `Escape`, outside click where unambiguous, and predictable focus return.
- On mobile, prioritize legibility over exact spatial anchoring.
- Do not animate individual words unless a specific passage and object justify it.

## Motion behavior

- Pointer movement produces small, damped depth changes rather than a floating camera.
- Foreground responds more than the house; the distant city responds least.
- Wind affects local plants and cloth asynchronously.
- Ambient motion settles or pauses when not visible.
- Interactive motion is reversible and returns cleanly to rest.
- Fast pointer movements do not cause exaggerated or delayed swings.
- Under reduced motion, preserve focus cues and state changes without parallax or continuous drift.

## Navigation

The first scene may serve as a provisional book landing page.

- Scene title and utility navigation remain quiet and subordinate.
- A road, path, or telegraph direction may later become the entrance to **The Journey**.
- Until the second scene exists, the exit remains visually complete but inactive or undisclosed; never use a dead button.

## Responsive behavior

### Wide desktop

- Preserve the full park-to-house relationship.
- Use anchored passage labels when space allows.
- Permit the richest layer count and subtle parallax.

### Tablet

- Reduce extreme foreground occlusion.
- Keep the house and primary hotspot targets within a stable central composition.

### Mobile portrait

- Use an authored crop, not the desktop composition shrunk down.
- Retain bench/house/threshold relationship even if the distant city is simplified.
- Increase target sizes without adding visible generic buttons.
- Use a composed passage sheet when anchored text would be cramped.
- Reduce layers, shaders, insects, and background motion before reducing passage legibility.

## Accessibility

- Every hotspot is keyboard focusable and has a concise accessible label.
- Focus indication fits the scene but remains clearly visible.
- Hover is never the only activation path.
- Passage panels trap focus only while modal and return it on close.
- Reduced motion removes parallax, slow cycling, scrubbed motion, and nonessential continuous animation.
- Contrast is measured on the actual rendered passage surface through all lighting states.
- Decorative images use empty alt text; meaningful scene descriptions receive concise alternatives.

## Sound

Optional, user-initiated, and muted by default.

Potential palette:

- Leaves and distant insects
- Quiet city resonance
- Paper and book movement
- Wire tension or faint telegraph-like vibration
- Wood and fabric

No continuous music is required. The scene must feel complete in silence.

## Renderer decision

Begin with layered raster elements, CSS perspective/transforms, semantic HTML, and one motion system.

Evaluate Three.js only if the blockout proves that one of these is essential:

- Occlusion or perspective that CSS layers cannot maintain
- Complex real-time light interaction
- A camera transition required to enter the courtyard or future road
- Particle or atmosphere behavior that cannot remain lightweight otherwise

A shader may have one localized responsibility. The base scene must remain complete without it.

## Acceptance criteria for the rough vertical slice

- The static frame reads as a distinctive place before any movement begins.
- The house, park, and waiting relationship remain legible on desktop and mobile.
- One passage interaction feels native to its object.
- Pointer, keyboard, touch, and reduced-motion paths all work.
- No generic startup UI or unexplained decorative effect is visible.
- Motion remains smooth and stops when the scene is hidden.
- The browser console has no relevant errors or failed assets.
- The implementation can be refined without replacing its entire structure.

## Out of scope for this scene

- Later plot events
- A historically exact city reconstruction
- Detailed character portraiture before consistency studies
- Full interior exploration of the house
- Game mechanics, inventory, achievements, or puzzles
- The complete cross-book library
- Public deployment
